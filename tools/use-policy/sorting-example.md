# Maple Street Neighbors: Our AI sort

Date: November 12, 2026

## Who is in the conversation

The director, two volunteers and the communications lead. Missing: a neighbor who uses the pantry.

## Our uses, sorted

| Use | Who benefits | Who carries the risk | Would the people most affected call it fair, safe and respectful? Who have we asked? | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draft the monthly newsletter from our own notes | Our volunteers, who save an evening | Neighbors, if a draft misstates a program | Probably yes. We have not asked yet. | Allowed |
| Summarize a case note in a free chatbot | The staff member, who saves ten minutes | The neighbor whose story is in the note | No. We would not want our own story pasted into a free tool. | Off limits |
| Turn volunteer sign-up sheets into a schedule | Our volunteer coordinator | Volunteers, if names are shared | Not sure. We will ask two volunteers. | Needs a pause |

## What we notice

Most uses that touch a neighbor's story landed in Off limits or Needs a pause. Uses that start from our own writing landed in Allowed.

## Who will see this before it becomes policy

Two neighbors who use the pantry and one elder from the church next door. We will ask them at the Saturday pantry and pay each of them for their time.
