# Maple Street Neighbors AI leadership commitment

**Draft: not yet ratified.** Date: November 19, 2026. In the conversation: director, board chair. Review date: May 19, 2027. Legal form: nonprofit. Size: small, with volunteers.

## What we commit to

We will keep AI use under the rules in our policy, and we will tell neighbors when it touches something about them.

## Who is accountable

- Strategy owner: the director. Reports to the board every quarter.
- Operations owner: the communications lead.
- Independent reviewer: a board member who does not administer our tools.

## Who holds each layer

- Policy: the director
- Oversight: the board chair
- Controls: the communications lead
- Audit: the independent reviewer
- Disclosure: the director

## How we will know it is working

A one-page report to the board each quarter: which tools we used, which uses we paused, and what neighbors told us.

## Who can pause a tool

The director or the board chair. Triggers: a neighbor reports harm, a vendor changes its data terms, or a staff member raises a concern. Turning a tool back on takes the director and one neighbor reviewer.

## Telling people

A neighbor is told in plain words when AI touched something about them. The board is told in the quarterly report.

## Raising a concern

Tell the director, or the board chair if it is about the director. No one is penalized for raising a concern.
