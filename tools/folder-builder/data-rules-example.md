# Maple Street Neighbors: Our data rules

Date: November 9, 2026

## Never goes into an AI tool

- Names, addresses or phone numbers of the people we serve
- Case notes or anything a neighbor told us in confidence
- Funder reports that include financial details

## May go in after names and details are removed

- Descriptions of our events
- Our own newsletter drafts

## What our tool keeps and brings back

- Chat history stays in our account until we delete it.
- Memory is turned off, so the tool brings nothing back between chats.
- The vendor's page says Business plan chats are not used for training. We asked the vendor to confirm in writing.

## Who checks AI work before it goes out

Named person: Communications lead

## Who to tell if something went in by mistake

Director
