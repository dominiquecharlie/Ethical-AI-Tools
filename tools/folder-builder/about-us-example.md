# Maple Street Neighbors: About us

Date: November 9, 2026 | Review date: February 9, 2027

## Who we serve

Families on the east side of our city, with a focus on neighbors who are new to the area.

## What we do

- Weekly neighbor meals and a volunteer-run clothing closet.
- A monthly newsletter that shares what is happening on our block.
- A volunteer schedule that keeps both programs open every week.

## Our purpose

Every neighbor on the block has someone to eat with and someone to call. (Example only. Your purpose is in your words.)

## Our rules

Neighbors decide what the block needs. We share credit. We say what we know and what we are still learning. (Short form of values.md.)

## How we talk

Warm, plain and direct. We write the way we talk at the meal table. (Short form of voice-profile.md.)

## Our past

Started by three neighbors with one shared table. The first meal fed twelve people. The closet came two years later when neighbors asked for it.

## Stories we tell

- The neighbor who came to one meal and now runs the Tuesday table. (Told with permission, no names.)

## How we present ourselves

Warm, plain and welcoming. Our colors are forest green and cream. Our logo appears on every flyer.

## Words our community uses

- "Neighbors" for the people we serve and the people who volunteer.
- "Block" for the area we work in.
