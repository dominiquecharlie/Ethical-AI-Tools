# Maple Street Neighbors: Our data rules

Date: November 9, 2026

## What goes in

- Shift dates, shift times and volunteer first names
- Our own newsletter drafts

## What the tool makes

- Weekly schedule tables
- Reminder messages to volunteers

## What is off limits

- Names, addresses or phone numbers of the people we serve
- Case notes or anything a neighbor told us in confidence

## What our vendor keeps and brings back

- The vendor keeps the messages our tool sends for 30 days, according to its page. We asked the vendor to confirm in writing.
- The vendor does not bring anything back between requests.

## Who checks output before it goes out

Named person: Volunteer coordinator

## Who to tell if something went in by mistake

Director

## Where each program's rules live

- volunteer-scheduling/data-rules.md
- newsletter/data-rules.md
