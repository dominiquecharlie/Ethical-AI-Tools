# Maple Street Neighbors: About us

Date: November 9, 2026 | Review date: February 9, 2027

## Who we serve

Families on the east side of our city, with a focus on neighbors who are new to the area.

## What we do

- Weekly neighbor meals and a volunteer-run clothing closet.
- A monthly newsletter that shares what is happening on our block.
- A volunteer schedule that keeps both programs open every week.

## How we present ourselves

Warm, plain and welcoming. Our colors are forest green and cream. Our logo appears on every flyer.

## Words our community uses

- "Neighbors" for the people we serve and the people who volunteer.
- "Block" for the area we work in.
